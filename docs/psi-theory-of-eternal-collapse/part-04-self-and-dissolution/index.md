# Part IV: Self and Dissolution

> *"Who dissolves whom when the dissolver is the dissolved?"*

## The Paradox of Dissolving Identity

At the heart of eternal collapse lies the ultimate paradox: the self that collapses is both agent and patient of its own dissolution. This part explores the grammatology of disappearance—how identity persists through, not despite, its own disintegration.

## Key Concepts Introduced

- **Dissolution Grammar**: The linguistic structures of self-reference and disappearance
- **Fragment Identity**: How coherent self emerges from incoherent pieces
- **Collapse Consciousness**: Awareness that survives its own dissolution
- **Interface Theory**: ψ as the boundary between being and non-being
