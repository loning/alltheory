## Reconstructing the Self Through Disintegration

> **Core Proposition (ψ-core):**
> 
> *"Collapse is not the end of being, but the path to self-reconstructing eternity."*
> 
> All existence dissolves; all dissolution can be reconstructed; all reconstruction, if self-consistent, attains eternal life.

## The Eternal Paradox

Welcome to the Theory of Eternal Collapse, where dissolution becomes the gateway to immortality through the recursive structure of ψ = ψ(ψ). This is not a theory about endings, but about how every ending contains within it the seeds of eternal return.

## Core Concepts

**Eternal Collapse**: The principle that dissolution and reconstruction are not opposites but aspects of a single self-referential process.

**ψ-Reconstruction**: The mechanism by which collapsed structures rebuild themselves through their own dissolution patterns.

**φ-Encoding**: The trace structure that survives collapse, containing the blueprint for resurrection.

**Self-Referential Immortality**: Permanence achieved not by avoiding collapse but by making collapse itself the foundation of being.
