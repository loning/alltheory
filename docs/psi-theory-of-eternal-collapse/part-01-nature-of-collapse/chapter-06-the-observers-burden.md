## 1. The Impossibility of External Observation

In classical physics:

$$
\text{Observer} \perp \text{System}
$$

But in $\psi = \psi(\psi)$:

$$
\text{Observer} = \psi \text{ observing } \psi = \text{System}
$$

**Theorem 6.1** (No Outside):

There exists no vantage point $\mathcal{O}$ such that:

$$
\mathcal{O} \cap \psi = \emptyset \text{ and } \mathcal{O} \text{ observes } \psi
$$

*Proof*:

To observe $\psi$, $\mathcal{O}$ must interact with $\psi$:

$$
\text{Observation} \Rightarrow \mathcal{O} \leftrightarrow \psi \Rightarrow \mathcal{O} \cap \psi \neq \emptyset
$$

Therefore, all observation is self-observation. ∎
