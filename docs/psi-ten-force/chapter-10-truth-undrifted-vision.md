## Why "Objective Truth" Is Impossible

**The Measurement Paradox**: To know objective truth, consciousness must observe without affecting. But observation IS consciousness affecting.

**Theorem 10.5** (Observer Limitation): For any observation O of system S by consciousness C: O = f(S, C), never just f(S).

*Proof*: Observation requires interaction. Interaction modifies both observer and observed. Pure S cannot be known, only S-as-experienced-by-C. "Objective" truth would require observation without observer — logical impossibility. ∎

This doesn't mean "no truth exists" — it means truth is always relational. The highest truth is consciousness knowing this limitation and including it in self-reflection.
