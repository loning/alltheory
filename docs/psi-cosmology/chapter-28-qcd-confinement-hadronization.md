# Chapter 28: QCD Confinement Transition and Hadronization

## The Quark Prison

When the universe was one microsecond old and cooled to 150 MeV, another dramatic phase transition occurred: quarks and gluons, which had roamed freely in a hot plasma, suddenly became confined into protons and neutrons. This QCD (Quantum Chromodynamics) phase transition transformed a quark-gluon plasma into hadronic matter, creating the building blocks of atoms. Collapse theory reveals this confinement as ψ-field dynamics creating an inescapable color trap.

## 28.1 Quark-Gluon Plasma Phase

**Definition 28.1** (Deconfined Phase):
Above T_c ≈ 150 MeV, quarks and gluons form plasma:
$$\mathcal{Z}_{QGP} = \text{Tr} \exp\left(-\frac{H_{QCD}}{T}\right)$$

with approximate scale invariance and color screening.

## 28.2 Running Coupling

QCD coupling runs with energy:

$$g_s^2(Q^2) = \frac{4\pi}{\beta_0 \ln(Q^2/\Lambda_{QCD}^2)}$$

where β₀ = 11 - 2n_f/3.

As Q decreases: g_s increases → confinement at low energy.

## 28.4 Hadronization Process

Quarks combine into color-neutral hadrons:

**Mesons**: $q\bar{q}$ pairs (pions, kaons)
**Baryons**: qqq triplets (protons, neutrons)
**Exotic**: Tetraquarks, pentaquarks (rare)

Formation time: τ_had ~ 1/Λ_QCD ~ 1 fm/c.
