## 24.1 Singularity Formation

**Definition 24.1** (Collapse Singularity):
A singularity forms where:
$$\lim_{r \to r_s} \psi(r) = \infty$$

with finite integrated mass:
$$M = \int_0^{r_s} 4\pi r^2 \rho_{\psi} dr < \infty$$

## 24.2 Event Horizon Emergence

The horizon appears where collapse velocity equals light speed:

$$v_{collapse} = \sqrt{\frac{2GM_{\psi}(r)}{r}} = c$$

yielding Schwarzschild radius:
$$r_s = \frac{2GM}{c^2}$$

Inside, all trajectories lead to singularity.

## 24.3 Interior Solution

**Theorem 24.1** (Black Hole Interior):
Inside the horizon, the collapse field follows:
$$\psi(r,t) = \frac{\psi_0}{(t_s - t)^{2/3}}\left(\frac{r_s}{r}\right)^{3/2}$$

where t_s is singularity time.

*Proof*:
Self-similar collapse solution of field equations. Power laws ensure finite mass with divergent density. Time dependence follows from dimensional analysis. ∎
