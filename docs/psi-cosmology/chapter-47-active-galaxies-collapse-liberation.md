# Chapter 47: Active Galaxies - Collapse Energy Liberation

## 47.1 Cosmic Beacons: When Black Holes Feast

Active galactic nuclei (AGN) represent the universe's most powerful persistent energy sources—supermassive black holes converting infalling matter into radiation with efficiencies that dwarf stellar fusion. These cosmic lighthouses can outshine entire galaxies, launch jets spanning millions of light-years, and regulate galaxy evolution.

**Definition 47.1** (AGN Luminosity): An active galaxy exhibits:
$$L_{AGN} = ε\dot{M}c^2$$

where:
- ε ~ 0.06-0.42 (radiative efficiency)
- $\dot{M}$ = accretion rate onto SMBH
- Can reach $L_{AGN} > 10^{48}$ erg/s

## 47.2 The Central Engine: Accretion Physics

AGN power derives from accretion onto supermassive black holes:

**Theorem 47.1** (Eddington Limit): Maximum luminosity for spherical accretion:
$$L_{Edd} = \frac{4πGMm_pc}{σ_T} ≈ 1.3 × 10^{38} \left(\frac{M}{M__\odot}\right) erg/s$$

where radiation pressure balances gravity.

*Proof*: From force balance on infalling proton... ∎

## 47.3 Accretion Disk Structure: Organized Infall

Matter forms a disk before falling into the black hole:

**Definition 47.2** (Shakura-Sunyaev Disk):
$$T(R) = \left[\frac{3GM\dot{M}}{8πσR^3}\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{R_{in}}{R}}\right)\right]^{1/4}$$

Disk regions:
1. **Outer disk**: R > 10³ R_s, geometrically thin
2. **Middle disk**: Radiation pressure dominated
3. **Inner disk**: R ~ few R_s, relativistic effects
4. **ISCO**: R = 6GM/c² (non-rotating BH)

## 47.4 AGN Classification: The Unified Model

Different AGN types reflect viewing angle and accretion rate:

**Theorem 47.2** (Unified Scheme): Observable properties depend on:
- Viewing angle θ to jet/disk axis
- Eddington ratio λ = L/L_Edd
- Black hole mass M_BH
- Spin parameter a

Type 1 AGN (face-on):
- Broad emission lines
- Blue continuum
- Variable on short timescales

Type 2 AGN (edge-on):
- Narrow lines only
- Obscured continuum
- Polarized broad lines

## 47.5 Quasars: The Brightest Lights

Quasars represent extreme AGN activity:

**Definition 47.3** (Quasar Properties):
- L_bol > 10⁴⁵ erg/s
- M_BH ~ 10⁸-10¹⁰ M__\odot
- z_peak ~ 2-3 (cosmic noon)
- Space density ~ 1 per Gpc³

Spectral features:
- Blue continuum: F_ν ∝ ν^(-0.5)
- Broad lines: v ~ 5000 km/s
- Fe II emission
- Big blue bump (UV)

## 47.6 Radio Galaxies: Jet Dominated

Some AGN launch powerful radio jets:

**Theorem 47.3** (Jet Power): Blandford-Znajek mechanism extracts:
$$P_{jet} = \frac{k}{4πc}Φ_{BH}²Ω_F²$$

where:
- Φ_BH = √(GM)B_H (magnetic flux)
- Ω_F = field angular frequency
- k ~ 0.05 (efficiency factor)

Jet properties:
- Lorentz factors: Γ ~ 10-50
- Collimation: θ_jet ~ 1/Γ
- Composition: e±, p+, or Poynting?
- Termination: lobes/hotspots

## 47.7 Seyfert Galaxies: Local AGN

Nearby spiral galaxies often host low-luminosity AGN:

**Definition 47.4** (Seyfert Classification):
- Type 1: Broad + narrow lines
- Type 1.5-1.9: Intermediate
- Type 2: Narrow lines only

Nuclear properties:
- L ~ 10⁴²-10⁴⁵ erg/s
- M_BH ~ 10⁶-10⁸ M__\odot
- Variable on hours-years
- ~10% of spirals

## 47.8 Blazars: Beamed Emission

When jets point at Earth, extreme properties emerge:

**Theorem 47.4** (Relativistic Beaming): Observed flux enhanced:
$$F_{obs} = δ^{3+α} F_{intrinsic}$$

where Doppler factor:
$$δ = \frac{1}{Γ(1-β\cos θ)}$$

Results in:
- Rapid variability (minutes)
- High polarization
- Superluminal motion
- TeV gamma rays

## 47.9 AGN Feedback: Galaxy Regulation

AGN regulate their host galaxies:

**Definition 47.5** (Feedback Modes):
1. **Radiative mode**: High accretion
   - Radiation pressure
   - Line driving
   - Compton heating

2. **Kinetic mode**: Low accretion
   - Jet heating
   - Bubble inflation
   - Turbulence driving

Energy coupling:
$$E_{coupled} = ε_f × E_{AGN} × t_{AGN}$$

## 47.10 Torus and Obscuration: Hidden Engines

A dusty torus obscures many AGN:

**Theorem 47.5** (Torus Properties):
$$N_H = n_0 R_{torus} f_{covering}$$

where:
- N_H ~ 10²²-10²⁴ cm⁻² (column density)
- R_torus ~ 1-10 pc
- f_covering ~ 0.5-0.7

Torus characteristics:
- Clumpy structure
- IR reprocessing
- Molecular gas
- Warped geometry

## 47.11 AGN Variability: Probing Spacetime

AGN vary on multiple timescales:

**Definition 47.6** (Variability Timescales):
- Minutes: Jet knots (blazars)
- Hours: Inner disk (X-rays)
- Days: Broad line region
- Months: Torus response
- Years: Radio jets

Reverberation mapping:
$$τ = \frac{R_{BLR}}{c}$$

yields black hole masses via:
$$M_{BH} = f × \frac{R_{BLR}v²}{G}$$

## 47.12 X-ray Coronae: Hot Electrons

Hard X-rays reveal hot coronae above disks:

**Theorem 47.6** (Compton Corona): Spectrum from:
$$\frac{dN}{dE} = N_0 E^{-Γ} e^{-E/E_c}$$

where:
- Γ ~ 1.5-2.5 (photon index)
- E_c ~ 100-300 keV (cutoff)
- Reflection features (Fe Kα)

Corona properties:
- T_e ~ 10⁹ K
- τ ~ 1 (optical depth)
- Compact: R < 10 R_g
- Variable power
