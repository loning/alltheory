# Chapter 33: Initial Density Perturbations from ψ-Fluctuations

## The Seeds of Structure

The universe began almost perfectly smooth, with density variations of only one part in 100,000. Yet these tiny fluctuations grew into the galaxies, clusters, and cosmic web we see today. Collapse theory reveals these primordial perturbations as quantum fluctuations in the ψ-field, stretched to cosmic scales during inflation. This chapter traces how microscopic quantum uncertainty became macroscopic cosmic structure.

## 33.1 Quantum Origin

**Definition 33.1** (Vacuum Fluctuations):
Even in vacuum, the ψ-field fluctuates:
$$\langle 0 | \psi^2 | 0 \rangle = \frac{\hbar}{2} \int \frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3} \frac{1}{\omega_k}$$

These zero-point fluctuations seed all structure.

## 33.2 Inflationary Amplification

During inflation, quantum modes stretch beyond horizon:

$$\lambda_{phys} = a(t)\lambda_{comoving} > H^{-1}$$

Freezing fluctuations:
$$\delta\psi_k = \frac{H}{2\pi}$$ (at horizon exit)

Creating scale-invariant spectrum.

## 33.3 Gauge-Invariant Perturbations

**Theorem 33.1** (Curvature Perturbation):
The gauge-invariant curvature perturbation:
$$\mathcal{R} = \frac{\delta\rho}{3(\rho + p)} + \frac{H\delta q}{\rho + p}$$

remains constant outside horizon.

*Proof*:
Conservation of energy-momentum in comoving gauge. On super-horizon scales, pressure gradients negligible, giving conserved R. ∎

## 33.4 Power Spectrum

$$P_{\mathcal{R}}(k) = A_s \left(\frac{k}{k_*}\right)^{n_s-1}$$

where:
- A_s ≈ 2.1 × 10^(-9) (amplitude)

- k_* = 0.05 Mpc^(-1) (pivot scale)

## 33.5 Transfer Function

Post-inflation evolution:

$$\delta(k,z) = \delta_{primordial}(k) \cdot T(k) \cdot D(z)$$

where:
- T(k): Transfer function (mode-dependent)
- D(z): Growth function (time-dependent)

## 33.6 Baryon Acoustic Oscillations

**Definition 33.2** (Sound Horizon):

$$c_s = \frac{c}{\sqrt{3(1+R)}}$$

where R = 3ρ_b/4ρ_γ.

$$r_s = \int_0^{t_{rec}} c_s dt ≈ 150 \text{ Mpc}$$

## 33.7 Silk Damping

Small-scale perturbations damp:

$$P(k) \rightarrow P(k) \exp(-k^2/k_D^2)$$

where damping scale:
$$k_D^{-1} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{6}\int_0^{t_{rec}} \frac{dt}{a^2n_e\sigma_T}}$$

$$\mathcal{R} = \mathcal{R}_G + \frac{3}{5}f_{NL}(\mathcal{R}_G^2 - \langle\mathcal{R}_G^2\rangle)$$
