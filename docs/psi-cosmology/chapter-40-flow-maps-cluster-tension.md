**Definition 40.1** (Collapse Tension Tensor):

$$T^{\mu\nu} = \rho u^\mu u^\nu + P g^{\mu\nu} + \Pi^{\mu\nu}$$

where ρ is collapse density, u^μ is flow velocity, P is pressure, and Π^μν is anisotropic stress from ψ-gradients.
