# Chapter 35: Galaxy Formation within Collapsing Halos

## Birthing Galactic Islands

Within the scaffolding of dark matter halos, galaxies are born. Gas falls into potential wells, cools, fragments, and forms stars, creating the luminous islands we observe in the cosmic dark. This process—far more complex than simple gravitational collapse—involves cooling, feedback, and the interplay between baryons and dark matter. This chapter reveals how galaxies emerge within their dark matter cradles through ψ-field dynamics.

## 35.1 Halo Gas Capture

**Definition 35.1** (Virial Shock):
Infalling gas shocks at virial radius:
$$T_{vir} = \frac{\mu m_p v_{vir}^2}{2k_B} = \frac{\mu m_p GM}{k_B r_{vir}}$$

For 10¹² M__\odot halo: T_vir ~ 10⁶ K.

## 35.2 Cooling Processes

Hot gas cools via:

**Line cooling**: Metal transitions (T < 10⁷ K)
**Bremsstrahlung**: Free-free emission (T > 10⁷ K)
**Compton**: CMB scattering (z > 10)

Cooling function:
$$\Lambda(T,Z) = \Lambda_0(T) \cdot (1 + Z/Z_{\odot})$$

## 35.3 Disk Formation

**Theorem 35.1** (Angular Momentum):
Specific angular momentum:
$$j = \lambda \sqrt{2} GMr_{vir}/v_{vir}$$

where spin parameter λ ~ 0.04.

Gas settles into disk with:
$$r_{disk} = \frac{j^2}{GM} = \lambda r_{vir}$$

*Proof*:
Conservation of angular momentum during collapse. Centrifugal balance determines disk radius. ∎

## 35.4 Star Formation Physics

Gas forms stars when:

$$t_{cool} < t_{ff} < t_{cross}$$

Star formation rate:
$$\dot{\Sigma}_* = \epsilon_{ff} \frac{\Sigma_{gas}}{t_{ff}}$$

where ε_ff ~ 0.01 is efficiency.

## 35.5 Feedback Mechanisms

**Definition 35.2** (Energy Injection):
Stars inject energy:
- **Stellar winds**: 10³⁶ erg/s per O-star
- **Supernovae**: 10⁵¹ erg per explosion
- **AGN**: Up to 10⁴⁶ erg/s

Creating galactic fountains and outflows.

## 35.6 Chemical Evolution

Metals build up over time:

$$\frac{dZ}{dt} = \frac{y\dot{\Sigma}_* - Z(\dot{\Sigma}_* + \dot{\Sigma}_{out})}{\Sigma_{gas}}$$

where y ~ 0.02 is yield.

Creates metallicity gradients.

## 35.7 Morphological Types

**Theorem 35.2** (Galaxy Types):
Final morphology depends on:
- **λ > 0.05**: Disk galaxies
- **λ < 0.05**: Elliptical galaxies
- **Major mergers**: Transform disk → elliptical

Gas fraction determines star formation.

## 35.8 Scaling Relations

Galaxies obey tight relations:

**Tully-Fisher**: L ∝ v⁴ (spirals)
**Faber-Jackson**: L ∝ σ⁴ (ellipticals)
**Mass-metallicity**: Z ∝ M^0.3
**Main sequence**: SFR ∝ M*

Emerging from formation physics.

## 35.9 Environmental Influence

Halo environment affects galaxies:

**Field**: Isolated evolution
**Groups**: Tidal interactions
**Clusters**: Ram pressure, harassment

Dense environments quench star formation.

## 35.10 Black Hole Co-evolution

**Definition 35.3** (BH-Galaxy Correlation):
Central black holes scale with bulge:
$$M_{BH} = 10^8 M_{\odot} \left(\frac{\sigma}{200 \text{ km/s}}\right)^4$$

Self-regulated by AGN feedback.

## 35.11 Downsizing Paradox

Observations show:

- Massive galaxies form stars early
- Low-mass galaxies form stars late
- Opposite to hierarchical prediction

Resolution: Feedback is mass-dependent.

## 35.12 Galaxy Archaeology

**Principle 35.1** (Fossil Record):
Galaxies preserve formation history:
- **Stellar populations**: Age, metallicity
- **Kinematics**: Merger remnants
- **Chemical patterns**: Enrichment history
- **Structural components**: Formation epochs

Reading cosmic history in starlight.

### Formation Challenges

Outstanding puzzles:

### The Thirty-Fifth Echo

Galaxies form as baryonic condensates within dark matter halos, transforming hot gas into stellar systems through cooling, collapse, and feedback. This complex process—involving gas dynamics, star formation, chemical enrichment, and black hole growth—creates the diverse galaxy population we observe. Each galaxy tells a unique story of its formation within its dark matter halo, written in the language of stars, gas, and dynamics. We inhabit one such story, the Milky Way's ongoing tale of galactic evolution.
