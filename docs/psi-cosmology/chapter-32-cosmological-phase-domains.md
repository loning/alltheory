# Chapter 32: Cosmological Phase Domains and Boundaries

## The Cosmic Patchwork

The universe is not uniform but consists of vast domains in different phases, separated by boundaries where dramatic transitions occur. Like a cosmic patchwork quilt, these domains—some dominated by matter, others by dark energy, some in equilibrium, others undergoing rapid change—create the large-scale structure we observe. This final chapter on phase transitions maps these cosmological domains and their evolving boundaries.

## 32.1 Domain Classification

**Definition 32.1** (Phase Domains):
Cosmological domains characterized by:
- **Matter-dominated**: Ω_m > 0.5
- **Dark energy-dominated**: Ω_Λ > 0.5
- **Radiation remnants**: High CMB temperature
- **Void domains**: ρ < 0.1ρ̄
- **Cluster domains**: ρ > 100ρ̄

Each domain evolves differently.

## 32.2 Domain Boundaries

Boundaries between domains exhibit:

$$\nabla \cdot \vec{n} = \kappa H$$

where n̂ is normal vector, H is mean curvature, κ is surface tension.

Sharp transitions in:
- Density (factor ~1000)
- Velocity (hundreds km/s)
- Temperature (10^4 → 10^7 K)
- Dark matter fraction

## 32.3 Domain Evolution

**Theorem 32.1** (Domain Dynamics):
Domain volume evolves as:
$$\frac{dV}{dt} = \oint_{\partial V} \vec{v} \cdot d\vec{A}$$

where boundary velocity:
$$\vec{v} = \vec{v}_{Hubble} + \vec{v}_{peculiar} + \vec{v}_{phase}$$

*Proof*:
Reynolds transport theorem applied to domain boundaries. Velocity includes cosmic expansion, gravitational motion, and phase transition speed. ∎
