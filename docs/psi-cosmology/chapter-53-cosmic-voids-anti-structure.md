# Chapter 53: Cosmic Voids - Anti-Structure Regions

## 53.1 The Empty Fullness: Where Collapse Failed

Cosmic voids represent the universe's negative space—vast regions where matter density falls far below average, where galaxies are rare, and where expansion dominates over gravitational collapse. These "anti-structures" occupy most of the universe's volume while containing minimal mass, serving as the photographic negative of the cosmic web.

**Definition 53.1** (Void Classification):
$$δ_v = \frac{ρ_v - \bar{ρ}}{\bar{ρ}} < -0.8$$

Void properties:
- Radius: R_v ~ 10-100 Mpc
- Volume fraction: f_v ~ 50-80%
- Mass fraction: f_m < 10%
- Interior galaxies: Sparse, blue, low-mass

## 53.2 Void Formation: Gravitational Evacuation

Voids form through matter outflow to surrounding structures:

**Theorem 53.1** (Void Evolution): Linear growth:
$$δ_v(t) = -\frac{D(t)}{3} × ∇·v_0$$

where initial velocity divergence ∇·v_0 > 0.

*Proof*: From continuity equation in expanding background... ∎

Non-linear evolution:
$$\frac{da_v}{dt} = H_0 a_v \sqrt{Ω_m a_v^{-3}(1 + δ_v) + Ω_Λ}$$

Leading to accelerated expansion within voids.

## 53.3 Void Profiles: Density Structure

Voids exhibit characteristic density profiles:

**Definition 53.2** (Universal Void Profile):
$$ρ(r) = ρ_c + (ρ_e - ρ_c) × \left(1 - \frac{r}{R_v}\right)^α$$

where:
- ρ_c = central density (minimum)
- ρ_e = edge density
- α ~ 2-3 (profile slope)
- R_v = void radius

Integrated underdensity:
$$Δ_v = \frac{3}{R_v³} ∫_0^{R_v} δ(r) r² dr ≈ -0.9$$

## 53.4 Void Hierarchy: Multi-Scale Structure

Voids exhibit hierarchical organization:

**Theorem 53.2** (Void-in-Void): Subvoid probability:
$$P(R_sub|R_parent) ∝ \left(\frac{R_sub}{R_parent}\right)^{-γ} × θ(R_parent - R_sub)$$

with γ ~ 2.5-3.

Creates:
- Supervoids: R > 100 Mpc
- Regular voids: R ~ 10-50 Mpc
- Minivoids: R < 10 Mpc
- Void hierarchy: Tree structure

## 53.5 Galaxies in Voids: Isolated Evolution

Void galaxies evolve differently:
