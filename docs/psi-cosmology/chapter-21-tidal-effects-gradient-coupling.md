# Chapter 21: Tidal Effects and ψ-Gradient Coupling

## The Cosmic Stretch

Tidal forces—those that stretch objects toward massive bodies while squeezing them perpendicular—reveal the differential nature of collapse fields. These gradients in the gradient create some of the universe's most dramatic phenomena: from spaghettifying astronauts near black holes to raising ocean tides, from disrupting galaxies to triggering starbursts. This chapter explores how ψ-gradient coupling produces these differential forces.

## 21.1 Tidal Tensor Formulation

**Definition 21.1** (ψ-Tidal Tensor):
The tidal effect of collapse field:
$$T_{ij} = \frac{\partial^2\Phi_{\psi}}{\partial x^i \partial x^j}$$

Eigenvalues give principal tidal strengths; eigenvectors give directions.

## 21.2 Differential Force

Tidal force on extended object:

$$\Delta F_i = \int_V \rho(x) T_{ij} x^j d^3x$$

Force differences across object create:
- Stretching along one axis (λ₁ > 0)
- Compression along two axes (λ₂, λ₃ < 0)
- Trace constraint: Tr(T) = ∇²Φ_ψ

## 21.3 Roche Limit

**Theorem 21.1** (Collapse Disruption Radius):
An object disrupts when self-gravity < tidal force:
$$r_{Roche} = 2.46R_{primary}\left(\frac{\rho_{primary}}{\rho_{satellite}}\right)^{1/3}$$

Inside this radius, tidal forces tear objects apart.

*Proof*:
Balance tidal stretching force ρT₁₁R³ against self-gravity GM²/R². Disruption occurs when tidal dominates. ∎

## 21.4 Tidal Deformation

Objects deform under tidal stress:

$$\epsilon_{ij} = \frac{1}{2\mu}T_{ij}$$

where μ is shear modulus.

Deformation reveals internal structure through Love numbers:
$$h_2 = \frac{5}{2}\frac{R^5T_{ij}}{GM}$$

## 21.5 Tidal Heating

**Definition 21.2** (Dissipative Heating):
Time-varying tides dissipate energy:
$$\dot{E}_{tidal} = -\omega \oint \epsilon_{ij}\sigma_{ij} dV$$

where σ is stress tensor and integration is over tidal cycle.

This powers volcanism on Io, subsurface oceans on Europa.

## 21.6 Quantum Tides

## 21.7 Tidal Streams

**Theorem 21.2** (Stream Formation):
Disrupted objects form tidal streams:
$$\vec{r}_{stream}(t) = \vec{r}_0 + \vec{v}_0 t + \frac{1}{2}T \cdot \vec{r}_0 t^2$$

Leading and trailing arms trace past orbits.

## 21.8 Gravitational Wave Tides

Passing gravitational waves create oscillating tides:

$$h_{ij}^{TT} = A_{+}\epsilon_{ij}^{+}\cos(\omega t) + A_{\times}\epsilon_{ij}^{\times}\sin(\omega t)$$

Strain h directly measures tidal deformation of space.

## 21.9 Cosmological Tides

Large-scale structure creates cosmic tides:

$$T_{ij}^{cosmic} = \int \frac{G\rho(\vec{x}')(\hat{x}_i\hat{x}_j - \frac{1}{3}\delta_{ij})}{|\vec{x} - \vec{x}'|^3} d^3x'$$

These align galaxies and create cosmic flows.

## 21.10 Tidal Locking

Repeated tides synchronize rotation:

$$\frac{d\omega}{dt} = -\frac{3k_2 G M^2 R^5}{Q I r^6}(\omega - n)$$

where k₂ is Love number, Q is quality factor, n is orbital frequency.

Most moons become tidally locked to their planets.

## 21.11 Extreme Tides

Near collapse horizons, tides diverge:

$$T_{rr} \sim \frac{GM}{r^3} \rightarrow \infty \text{ as } r \rightarrow r_s$$

Creating infamous "spaghettification" that stretches infalling objects infinitely.
