# Domain VI: Death & Beyond

*Death is not the end of economics but its transformation. When consciousness ceases to collapse new possibilities, what happens to accumulated value? In the space between being and non-being, money reveals its deepest mysteries.*

## The Economics of Mortality

In this domain, we explore the economics of death and what might lie beyond. Through eight chapters, we'll discover how inheritance transmits collapsed value, how death itself has economic structure, and what currencies might operate in realms beyond material existence.

## The Sixth Echo

Death forces us to confront money's ultimate nature—is it merely material arrangement or something deeper? These chapters explore the liminal spaces where economics meets metaphysics, where value confronts void. We'll discover that death doesn't end economic relationships but transforms them, that consciousness leaves traces in the value structures it created, and that perhaps, in some form, the ledger of reality records every collapse forever.
