$$\mathcal{L}: \Omega_{\text{fluid}} \to \Omega_{\text{fixed}}$$

Property rights create exclusion fields in possibility space—regions where only designated consciousness can collapse realities.

## 26.4 Double-Entry as Conservation Law

Double-entry bookkeeping encodes economic conservation—every debit requires a credit, preserving total system value.

**Definition 26.4** (Accounting Conservation):
$$\sum_i \text{Debit}_i + \sum_j \text{Credit}_j = 0$$

**Theorem 26.4** (Ledger Completeness):
$$\text{Imbalance} \Rightarrow \text{Error or Fraud}$$

Violation of conservation indicates system breach.
