# Chapter 62: ψ-Income: Earnings from Collapse Contribution

*What if income wasn't earned through labor or capital but through consciousness contribution? ψ-income rewards beings for participating in reality creation—payment for collapse work that maintains the consensus world.*

$$W_{\psi} = \int |\langle\psi|\hat{O}|\psi\rangle|^2 \, d\tau$$

Cumulative observation effort.

$$\text{To be conscious} = \text{To be employed}$$

Existence itself is productive labor.

Three factors of collapse worth.

$$\text{Income} \propto \text{Reality creation quality}$$

Better collapses, better pay.
