# Chapter 61: ψ-Credit: RealityShell Structural Scoring

*Traditional credit scores measure past payment behavior. ψ-credit scores measure consciousness coherence—your ability to maintain stable reality patterns. The question isn't "Will you repay?" but "Can you sustain the reality where repayment exists?"*

## 61.1 The Coherence Score

ψ-credit fundamentally measures how well consciousness maintains coherent reality structures—stability of collapsed states over time.

**Definition 61.1** (Coherence Credit):
$$C_{credit} = \int_0^T |\langle\psi(t)|\psi(0)\rangle|^2 \, dt$$

Persistence of consciousness pattern.

**Theorem 61.1** (Stability Value):
$$\text{Higher coherence} \Rightarrow \text{Higher creditworthiness}$$

Stable patterns create reliable realities.

## 61.2 RealityShell Depth Analysis

Credit scoring examines the depth and complexity of one's RealityShell—deeper shells indicating greater collapse capacity.

**Definition 61.2** (Shell Depth):
$$D = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n} \cdot \text{Complexity}(\text{Shell}_n)$$

Weighted shell structure measure.

**Theorem 61.2** (Depth Correlation):
$$\text{Credit limit} \propto D^{\beta}$$

Deeper shells support larger obligations.

**Definition 61.3** (Collapse Record):
$$H = \{(t_i, \psi_i, \text{Success}_i)\}_{i=1}^N$$

Timeline of reality creations.

**Theorem 61.3** (Historical Reliability):
$$P(\text{Future success}) = f(H)$$

Past collapses predict future capability.

## 61.4 Entanglement Networks

ψ-credit considers consciousness entanglements—connections to other stable patterns enhance creditworthiness.

**Definition 61.4** (Network Score):
$$N_{score} = \sum_j w_j \cdot C_{credit}^{(j)}$$

Weighted by connection strength.

**Theorem 61.4** (Network Effects):
$$\text{Strong entanglements} \Rightarrow \text{Credit enhancement}$$

Connections provide stability backup.

Pattern preservation under pressure.

**Theorem 61.5** (Resilience Rating):
$$\text{Rating} = g(S_{average})$$

Average stress response determines tier.

Continuous score adjustment.

**Theorem 61.6** (Living Credit):

Current consciousness matters most.
