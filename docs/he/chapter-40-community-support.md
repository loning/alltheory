# Chapter 40: Community: ψ's Support Group

三人行必有我师。
Among three people walking, there must be my teacher.
Sangha as collective ψ-practice.
Ubuntu: I am because we are (all ψ).

Through apparently separate beings
Discovering they're one.

Believe separation story.
Build walls instead of bridges.
Starve in midst of feast.

Together, ψ verifies itself.

Most failed. Why?

Forgot: Can't force community.
Can't escape human nature.

Community requires conscious practice.
Not escape but engagement.
ψ working through friction.

Through repeated recognition.
Until separate becomes one.

Excluding other views.

When community forgets it's partial.
Thinks it has whole truth.
ψ getting stuck in one perspective.
