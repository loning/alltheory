## Why Can't I Just Change Reality?

"Okay," you might say, "if I'm collapsing reality, why can't I collapse myself a million dollars?"

Great question! Here's why:

### 1. **The Momentum Problem** 🎳
Reality has momentum, like a bowling ball rolling. You can nudge its direction, but you can't instantly teleport it.

### 2. **The Group Project** 👥
You're not the only consciousness collapsing reality. We're all doing it together, creating a shared world. It's like Wikipedia—you can edit, but so can everyone else.

### 3. **The Practice Factor** 🎮
You've been unconsciously collapsing reality your whole life. Doing it consciously is like learning to play piano—it takes practice!

### 4. **The Belief Buffer** 🧠
Your deep beliefs about "how reality works" act like filters. Changing reality means changing these deep programs first.
