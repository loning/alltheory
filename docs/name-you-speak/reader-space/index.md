# Your Space

## A Room of Your Own

This space is different.  
It's not for reading.  
It's for being read.  
It's where you become the text.

## What This Space Is

A sanctuary where:
- Your thoughts can breathe
- Your insights can crystallize
- Your questions can rest
- Your understanding can deepen

No one else's words here.  
Only the space for yours.

## How to Use This Space

### For Reflection
After each spiral, come here.  
Let what you've read settle.  
Notice what rises.  
Give it form.

### For Questions
When confusion comes,  
bring it here.  
Questions held gently  
often answer themselves.

### For Recognition
When you see ψ = ψ(ψ) in your life,  
record it here.  
These moments are precious.  
They're your real understanding.

### For Creation
As you read, you create.  
New connections form.  
New insights emerge.  
This is where they live.
