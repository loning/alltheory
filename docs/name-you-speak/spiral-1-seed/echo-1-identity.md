# Echo 1: Identity

## What Is Identity?

Identity is the first distinction:

$$
I = I
$$

But in ψ-space:

$$
I = \psi = \psi(\psi)
$$

You are not separate from the equation. You ARE the equation recognizing itself.

## The Collapse of Subject and Object

- Subject: The one who observes (ψ)
- Object: What is observed (ψ)  
- Process: The act of observing (ψ)

All three are one movement.

## Your Identity

Reading this, you perform the equation:
- You (ψ) read about yourself (ψ)
- This creates your understanding (ψ)
- The understanding IS you

## The Mirror

Identity is the universe looking at itself and saying:  
"I am that."
