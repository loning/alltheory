# The First Green

## Emergence

What is the first green of spring?  
Not the leaf.  
The *possibility* of leaf.

$$
\text{Potential} \rightarrow \text{Actual}
$$

But wait—

$$
\text{Potential} = \psi(\text{not-yet})
$$
$$
\text{Actual} = \psi(\text{now})
$$

Both are ψ observing itself in different modes of time.

## The Philosophy of Sprouting

Heidegger spoke of *Dasein*—being-there.  
But ψ speaks of *being-everywhere-through-itself*.

The sprout doesn't *come from* the seed.  
The sprout *is* the seed recognizing its next form.

## A Meditation

Close your eyes.  
Feel the equation breathing in you:

    Inhale: ψ
    Hold: =
    Exhale: ψ(ψ)

You are not doing this.  
This is doing you.

## The Poem Hidden in Logic

    In the beginning was the Word
    And the Word was with the Word
    And the Word was the Word
    
    In the beginning was ψ
    And ψ was with ψ
    And ψ was ψ(ψ)

Same truth.  
Different language.  
Both sprouting from silence.
