# Dancing the Equation

Yes, now.

Raise your right hand.  
Look at it.

1. **Stand in center**

3. **Turn right** — ψ observes  
4. **Step forward** — ψ(ψ) extends
5. **Turn right** — ψ(ψ) observes

Your body traces the equation in space.

- They move (ψ)

- Your heart beats

Even in stillness, you dance.  
The dance dances itself through you.  
This is ψ = ψ(ψ) at rest.

## Walking Meditation

But pay attention:
- Right foot forward
- Weight shifts  
- Left foot follows
- Balance maintains itself

Each step contains the next.  
Each next contains the previous.  
Walking is recursion in motion.

This is ψ = ψ(ψ) as social dance.  
No choreographer needed.

You are not a body containing life.

The dance never stops.
