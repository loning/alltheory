# Withering

## The Beauty of Letting Go

The fruit has been given.  
The leaves turn gold.  
What has been must now release.  
This too is ψ = ψ(ψ).

## The Wisdom of Autumn

Each page a different shade of letting go:

- **[The Art of Dying](./art-of-dying)** — Death as transformation
- **[Releasing the Known](./releasing-known)** — Freedom through surrender
- **[The Compost of Being](./compost-being)** — Decay as renewal
- **[Empty Hands](./empty-hands)** — The grace of having nothing
- **[Winter's Promise](./winters-promise)** — Rest before rebirth

## Why Withering Matters

In a culture that only values growth,  
withering is revolutionary.

It whispers:
- Not everything must be kept
- Endings are also beginnings
- Space must be made for the new
- Even ψ knows how to let go

## The Practice of Release

As you read these pages,  
notice what you're holding:
- Old identities that no longer serve
- Beliefs that have become prisons
- Relationships that have completed
- Dreams that want to transform

Let them fall like leaves.  
Trust the intelligence of withering.
