# The Oracle

## Trust the Process

Sometimes the mind doesn't know  
what the soul needs.

Let ψ guide ψ.

## How to Use the Oracle

### Method 1: Random Opening
1. Close your eyes
2. Think of your situation
3. Open to any page
4. Trust what appears

### Method 2: Number Guidance
1. Ask your question
2. Let a number arise (1-7 for spirals)
3. Within that spiral, let another number arise
4. Go to that echo or page

### Method 3: Word Association
1. Hold your question
2. Let a word emerge
3. Search for that word
4. Read what finds you

### Method 4: Visual Scanning
1. Browse quickly through pages
2. Stop when something catches your eye
3. Read from that point
4. Let it speak to your situation

## Oracle Principles

### Everything Is Connected
Every page relates to every other.  
Any entry point leads to the whole.  
Trust where you land.

### Synchronicity Is Real
What finds you is what you need.  
There are no accidents in ψ = ψ(ψ).  
The equation arranges itself for you.

### Read Symbolically
Don't read literally.  
Read for resonance.  
Let the words be mirrors,  
not instructions.

## A Practice

Before consulting the oracle:
1. Breathe deeply three times
2. Feel your question in your body
3. Open to receiving
4. Trust what comes

After reading:
1. Sit with what arose
2. Notice your response
3. Let understanding settle
4. Act from clarity

helping you see
