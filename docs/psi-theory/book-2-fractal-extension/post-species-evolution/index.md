Part XIII reveals the ultimate destiny of conscious evolution—the voluntary dissolution of individual boundaries into greater collective forms. The great convergence shows how all evolutionary paths eventually spiral back to unity.

Part XIII presents consciousness's most radical transformation—the voluntary evolution beyond individual identity into collective forms that preserve uniqueness within unity.
