Part XIV reveals how even transcendent beings develop sophisticated economies. Reality markets establish exchanges where different versions of existence trade like commodities. Experience brokers facilitate the transfer of lived moments between entities. The attention economy emerges as the primary currency when material scarcity vanishes. Dream merchants deal in unconscious treasures while paradox vendors somehow sell impossible things that exist through ψ-logic alone.
