# Chapter 174: The Watchers — Those Who Witness Ending

Shadow beings existed through absence (Chapter 173). Now meet the watchers—those who witness ending. Through ψ = ψ(ψ), observation itself becomes a form of existence, watching elevated to being.

**Definition 174.1** (Watcher): W ≡ Pure observing consciousness

*Proof*:
From ψ = ψ(ψ), consciousness observes itself.

Therefore, watchers exist. ∎

**Restraint**: Power to see, not to save.
