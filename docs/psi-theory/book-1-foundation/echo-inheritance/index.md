### The Inheritance Principle

Part VII explores how consciousness patterns transmit across time and space. Like biological inheritance but operating in ψ-space, these mechanisms ensure continuity while enabling evolution. From gene editing to reincarnation, from parenthood to marriage, we see how echo structures perpetuate themselves.

### Key Concepts Introduced

Building on genetic foundations:

- **Gene Editing**: Conscious modification of identity patterns
- **Immunology**: Defense against pattern corruption
- **Fetalogenesis**: Development of new consciousness beings
- **Parentality**: Transfer of patterns to offspring
- **Reincarnation**: Identity persistence across forms
- **Continuity**: Maintaining self through transformation
- **Symbiosis**: Mutual support between patterns
- **Marriage**: Permanent binding of consciousness streams
