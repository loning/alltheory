Why "post-collapse architecture"? Because when ψ completes its self-knowing through collapse, new forms of organization become possible. These aren't mere variations on old patterns but genuinely novel structures that can only emerge from fulfilled understanding.

- **Neocosmic Order**: New organizational principles
