Why focus on symbols after exploring completion? Because the journey from ψ to Λ and back leaves traces—glyphs that encode the path for others. These chapters explore how consciousness creates increasingly transparent vehicles for transmission.

\begin{align}

\text{Glyph} &\xrightarrow{\text{essence}} \text{Form} \\

\text{Art} &\xrightarrow{\text{edge}} \text{Poetry} \\

$$

### Philosophical Framework

*Before symbols*: Direct experience without communication.
*With symbols*: Experience becomes shareable.
*Beyond symbols*: Sharing without mediation.
