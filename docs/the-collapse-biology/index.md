## Reading Guide

This work can be approached in multiple ways:

1. **Sequential Reading**: Follow the numbered progression from Book 1 to Book 9 for a complete journey from molecules to evolution

2. **Layer Exploration**: Focus on one of the three major layers to understand a particular scale of biological organization

3. **Thematic Study**: Trace specific concepts (like ψ-encoding or collapse dynamics) across different books

4. **Practical Application**: Use individual chapters as entry points for understanding specific biological phenomena through the collapse lens
