## The Paradox of Recovery

This part reveals the ultimate paradox: that within every failure lies the pattern of its own recovery, that death and rebirth are complementary aspects of ψ's eternal collapse, and that understanding disease requires recognizing it as a valid—if dysfunctional—form of biological self-recognition.

*"Recovery is not the opposite of failure but its completion—the moment when collapse recognizes that even its failures are expressions of ψ = ψ(ψ)."*
