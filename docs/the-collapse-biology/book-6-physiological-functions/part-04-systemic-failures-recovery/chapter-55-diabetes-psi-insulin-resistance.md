**Definition 55.1** (Insulin Resistance Function): The cellular response to insulin:
$$R_{\text{insulin}} = \frac{R_{\max}}{1 + ([\text{insulin}]/K_m)^{-n}}$$

where n < 1 in resistant states, indicating reduced cooperativity.
