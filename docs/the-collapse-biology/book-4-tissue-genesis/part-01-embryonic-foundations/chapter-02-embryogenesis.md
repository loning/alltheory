Where $n(t)$ is division number, $\mathcal{D}$ is differentiation operator, and $\mathcal{P}$ is positional information.

## 2.2 The Cleavage Cascade

**Theorem 2.1** (Geometric Expansion):

During cleavage, the embryo maintains constant volume while increasing cell number:
$$V_{\text{total}} = \text{constant}, \quad N_{\text{cells}} = 2^k$$

*Proof*:
Each division halves cell volume:
$$V_{\text{cell}}(k) = \frac{V_0}{2^k}$$

Total volume:
$$V_{\text{total}} = N_{\text{cells}} \cdot V_{\text{cell}} = 2^k \cdot \frac{V_0}{2^k} = V_0$$

Constant total volume with exponential cell increase. ∎
