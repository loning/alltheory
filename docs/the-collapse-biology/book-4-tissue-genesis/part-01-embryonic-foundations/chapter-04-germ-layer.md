$$\psi_{\text{ecto}} \rightarrow \{\text{Neural}, \text{Epidermal}, \text{Sensory}\}$$

$$[\text{BMP}] < \text{threshold} \Rightarrow \text{Neural}$$
$$[\text{BMP}] > \text{threshold} \Rightarrow \text{Epidermal}$$
