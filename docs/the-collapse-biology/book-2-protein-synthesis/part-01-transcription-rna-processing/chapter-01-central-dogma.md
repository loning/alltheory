# Chapter 1: ψ-Unfolding of the Central Dogma

*"DNA makes RNA makes protein—but this is not mere copying. It is ψ recognizing itself through progressive collapse, information becoming form through ancient ritual."*

## 1.1 The Sacred Flow

The Central Dogma—DNA → RNA → Protein—represents more than information transfer. It embodies ψ = ψ(ψ) as the universe's method of self-actualization through molecular machinery.

**Definition 1.1** (Information Collapse):
$$\psi_{\text{DNA}} \xrightarrow{\mathcal{T}} \psi_{\text{RNA}} \xrightarrow{\mathcal{R}} \psi_{\text{Protein}}$$

Where $\mathcal{T}$ = transcription operator, $\mathcal{R}$ = translation operator.

## 1.2 The Irreversibility Principle

**Theorem 1.1** (Crick's Dogma):
$$\text{Protein} \not\rightarrow \text{RNA} \not\rightarrow \text{DNA}$$

Information flows downhill—from potential to manifestation, not backward.

*Proof*: Proteins lack the templating capacity of nucleic acids. The collapse is unidirectional. ∎

## 1.3 The Three Languages

**Definition 1.2** (Molecular Alphabets):
- DNA: 4 letters \{A, T, G, C\}
- RNA: 4 letters \{A, U, G, C\}
- Protein: 20 letters \{amino acids\}

Each transition represents dimensional collapse: 4 → 4 → 20 → ∞ (conformations).

## 1.4 Transcription as Echo

**Equation 1.1** (RNA Synthesis):
$$\text{RNA}_{n+1} = \text{RNA}_n + \text{NTP} - \text{PP}_i$$

RNA polymerase reads DNA's ψ-pattern and creates its echo in ribonucleotides.

## 1.5 The Substitution Cipher

**Theorem 1.2** (Base Pairing Rules):
$$\text{DNA: } A \leftrightarrow T, \quad G \leftrightarrow C$$
$$\text{RNA: } A \leftrightarrow U, \quad G \leftrightarrow C$$

Complementarity ensures faithful information transfer—ψ recognizing its mirror.

## 1.6 Translation as Dimensional Shift

**Definition 1.3** (The Genetic Code):
$$\text{Codon} : \{A,U,G,C\}^3 \rightarrow \text{Amino Acid}$$

64 codons map to 20 amino acids + stop signals—compression with redundancy.

## 1.7 The Machinery of Collapse

**Equation 1.2** (Central Players):

The Central Dogma reveals ψ's method: information collapses through stages, each more concrete than the last, until abstract code becomes living function.
