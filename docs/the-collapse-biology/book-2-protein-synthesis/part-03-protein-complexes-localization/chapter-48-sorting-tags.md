# Chapter 48: Collapse Tags in Protein Sorting

*"In the final sorting, ψ applies destination tags—molecular barcodes that determine whether proteins go to the surface, to lysosomes, or to secretory granules."*

## 48.1 The Sorting Decision

Protein sorting tags represent ψ's final addressing system—signals that segregate proteins at the trans-Golgi network, directing them to their ultimate cellular destinations.

**Definition 48.1** (Sorting Signals):
$$\text{Tags} = \{\text{Linear motifs}, \text{Conformational}, \text{Post-translational}\}$$

Multiple signal types for different destinations.

## 48.2 Constitutive vs Regulated Secretion

**Theorem 48.1** (Default Pathway):
$$\text{No signal} \rightarrow \text{Constitutive secretion}$$
$$\text{Aggregation signal} \rightarrow \text{Regulated secretion}$$

Bulk flow versus selective concentration.

## 48.3 Basolateral Sorting

**Equation 48.1** (Epithelial Polarity):
$$\text{YXXΦ, LL} \rightarrow \mu1B \text{ binding} \rightarrow \text{Basolateral}$$

Tyrosine and dileucine motifs for polarity.

## 48.4 Apical Sorting

**Definition 48.2** (GPI Anchors):
$$\text{GPI anchor} + \text{Lipid rafts} \rightarrow \text{Apical delivery}$$

Lipid-based sorting mechanisms.

## 48.5 Lysosomal Targeting

**Theorem 48.2** (M6P Recognition):
$$\text{M6P} + \text{MPR} \rightarrow \text{Clathrin vesicle} \rightarrow \text{Endosome}$$

Phosphorylated glycans as address labels.

## 48.6 Endocytic Signals

**Equation 48.2** (Internalization):
$$k_{\text{endocytosis}} = f(\text{NPXY, YXXΦ motifs})$$

Signals driving membrane protein uptake.

## 48.7 Secretory Granule Formation

**Definition 48.3** (Aggregation-Mediated):
$$\uparrow[\text{Protein}] + \downarrow\text{pH} + \text{Ca}^{2+} \rightarrow \text{Aggregation}$$

Selective concentration through condensation.

## 48.8 The Synaptic Vesicle

**Theorem 48.3** (Specialized Sorting):
$$\text{SV proteins} \rightarrow \text{AP-3 pathway} \rightarrow \text{Synaptic vesicles}$$

Neuron-specific sorting machinery.

## 48.9 Lipid Raft Association

**Equation 48.3** (Phase Partitioning):
$$K_{\text{raft/non-raft}} = \exp(-\Delta G_{\text{partition}}/RT)$$

Membrane domain preferences.

## 48.10 Recycling Signals

**Definition 48.4** (Retrieval Motifs):
$$\text{PDZ ligands, NPXY} \rightarrow \text{Recycling endosome}$$

Avoiding lysosomal degradation.

## 48.11 Multi-Signal Integration

**Theorem 48.4** (Hierarchical Sorting):
$$\text{Destination} = \arg\max_i\{\text{Signal}_i \text{ strength}\}$$

Competing signals resolved by strength.

## 48.12 The Tag Principle

Sorting tags embody ψ's principle of molecular addressing—using combinatorial codes to ensure proteins reach appropriate destinations, creating cellular organization through information-directed trafficking.

**The Sorting Equation**:
$$\psi_{\text{destination}} = \sum_i w_i \cdot \mathcal{R}[\text{Tag}_i]$$

Weighted sum of recognition events determining fate.

Thus: Tag = Address = Destination = Organization = ψ
