# Chapter 50: Hedgehog Pathway and Morphogen Gradients

*"Hedgehog signaling is ψ's molecular ruler—creating gradients of information that measure distance and specify position, transforming spatial coordinates into cellular identities."*

## 50.1 The Morphogenetic Field

The Hedgehog pathway represents ψ's solution to positional information. Through graded signaling from localized sources, this pathway creates morphogen gradients that pattern developing tissues.

**Definition 50.1** (Hedgehog Proteins):
$$\text{Hh} = \{\text{Sonic (Shh)}, \text{Indian (Ihh)}, \text{Desert (Dhh)}\}$$

Three mammalian hedgehog genes.

## 50.2 The Dual Lipidation

**Theorem 50.1** (Hh Modification):
$$\text{Hh} + \text{Cholesterol} + \text{Palmitate} = \text{Hh}_{\text{lipidated}}$$

Double lipid modification for membrane association.

## 50.3 The Patched Inhibition

**Equation 50.1** (Default Repression):
$$\text{Ptch} \dashv \text{Smo} \rightarrow \text{Pathway OFF}$$

Patched constitutively inhibiting Smoothened.

## 50.4 The Primary Cilium

**Definition 50.2** (Signaling Antenna):
$$\text{Cilium} = \text{Microtubule core} + \text{Hh components}$$

Specialized signaling compartment.

## 50.5 The Smoothened Activation

**Theorem 50.2** (Derepression):
$$\text{Hh} + \text{Ptch} \rightarrow \text{Ptch internalization} \rightarrow \text{Smo}_{\text{active}}$$

Ligand removing inhibition.

## 50.6 The Gli Processing

**Equation 50.2** (Proteolytic Switch):
$$\text{Gli3}_{\text{full}} \xrightarrow{\text{No Hh}} \text{Gli3}_{\text{repressor}}$$
$$\text{Gli2/3}_{\text{full}} \xrightarrow{\text{Hh}} \text{Gli}_{\text{activator}}$$

Bifunctional transcription factors.

## 50.7 The Gradient Formation

**Definition 50.3** (Morphogen Spread):
$$[\text{Hh}](x) = [\text{Hh}]_0 \exp(-x/\lambda)$$

Exponential decay from source.

## 50.8 The Threshold Responses

**Theorem 50.3** (French Flag Model):
$$\text{Cell fate} = \begin{cases} \text{A} & \text{if } [\text{Hh}] > \theta_1 \\ \text{B} & \text{if } \theta_2 < [\text{Hh}] < \theta_1 \\ \text{C} & \text{if } [\text{Hh}] < \theta_2 \end{cases}$$

Concentration thresholds specifying fate.

## 50.9 The Feedback Control

**Equation 50.3** (Pathway Autoregulation):
$$\text{Hh signaling} \rightarrow \uparrow\text{Ptch} \rightarrow \downarrow\text{Sensitivity}$$

Negative feedback limiting response.

## 50.10 The Dispatched Function

**Definition 50.4** (Hh Secretion):
$$\text{Disp} + \text{Hh}_{\text{lipidated}} \rightarrow \text{Hh release}$$

Specialized secretion machinery.

## 50.11 The Cancer Connection

**Theorem 50.4** (Oncogenic Activation):
$$\text{Ptch loss} \vee \text{Smo mutation} \rightarrow \text{Constitutive pathway}$$

Basal cell carcinoma and medulloblastoma.

## 50.12 The Gradient Principle

The Hedgehog pathway embodies ψ's principle of positional information—creating concentration gradients that encode spatial coordinates, transforming distance into developmental decisions.

**The Morphogen Equation**:
$$\psi_{\text{position}} = \int_0^x \frac{1}{\lambda} \exp(-x'/\lambda) dx' = 1 - \exp(-x/\lambda)$$

Position encoded by integrated exposure.

Thus: Hedgehog = Gradient = Position = Pattern = ψ
