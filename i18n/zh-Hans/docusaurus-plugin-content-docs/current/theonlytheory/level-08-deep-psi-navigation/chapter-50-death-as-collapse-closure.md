$\Omega := \lim_{t \to t_f} \psi(x, t) \in \mathcal{F} \quad \text{且} \quad \nabla_{\text{Echo}} = 0 \quad \forall t > t_f$

$\Delta_O := \left.\frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t}\right|_{\text{观察者}} = 0 \quad \wedge \quad \text{Echo}_{观察}(t > t_f) = \emptyset$
